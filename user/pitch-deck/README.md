# Pitch deck

`simplify-med-pitch-deck.pdf` is a seven-slide, under-one-minute pitch (1920×1080 pages):

1. Intro: “Medical paperwork, made clear.”
2. Problem: 57% / 51% / 58% survey findings; 200+ surveys, 50 patient interviews, 30 clinician interviews
3. Research: the gaps patients and clinicians both named, mapped to `simplify`, `prep` and `med-lit`
4. Solution: the grounding pipeline, AHRQ plain language, and code-checked output
5. Before and after: ChatGPT on the raw ER report vs. `simplify`
6. What's next: `prep`, `simplify`, `med-lit`, and the care circle
7. Thank you, contact details, and how to install

`before-chatgpt.png` and `after-simplify-med.png` are the screenshots used on slide 5 and in the root README.

Headline participant counts are rounded fieldwork totals. The percentages come from the connected, de-identified corpus in `user/data/`.
