# Visit Brief Template

Fill only sections with content; omit empty ones. Keep the whole brief short enough to read on a phone in the waiting room. Quote or closely follow the patient's words. Labels in brackets show origin and are required.

```markdown
# My visit brief

**Appointment:** <purpose in the patient's words> · <date/time if known> · <clinician or department if known> · <in person / telehealth if known>
**Prepared by:** <me / caregiver for NAME>

## My priorities
1. **<Top concern in my words>**
   - Started: <approximate onset as stated> · <sudden/gradual if stated>
   - Pattern: <how long / how often, if stated>
   - Effect on my life: <as stated>
   - Tried: <what and whether it helped, as stated>
2. **<Second concern>**
   - ...

**What I hope to get from this visit:** <patient's goal>

**Also mention if time allows:** <lower-priority concerns, if any>

## Before the appointment
- <Preparation step> [clinic instructions: <source>]
- Confirm with the clinic: <missing, unclear, or conflicting instruction>

## Things to bring
- [ ] <item> — **Required by clinic** (<source>)
- [ ] <item> — Optional

## Questions to ask
1. <Most important question>
2. ...

**If there is time**
- <topic question>

---
_Prepared from what I supplied. Questions are suggestions I reviewed. Not reviewed by a clinician._
```

## Origin rules

- `[clinic instructions: <source>]` marks anything taken from supplied appointment materials.
- Priorities and details are patient statements; do not tag each line, but never mix in generated content.
- Questions and optional items are suggestions; the footer says so.
- If no clinic instructions were supplied, replace "Before the appointment" with one line: "No clinic instructions were provided. Check your appointment message or call the clinic to confirm any preparation."
